About Me

Hello, I'm a junior at Texas Christian University, majoring in Digital Culture & Data Analytics. I was born in California and raised in Fort Worth, TX.

Before transferring to TCU, I worked full-time as a legal assistant for almost four years. During my time at Branch & Dhillon Law Firm, I became a certified Notary Public, assisted with drafting legal documents, and developed strong organizational and problem-solving skills. That experience taught me the importance of accuracy, communication, and attention to detail. I now use these skills in data analytics.

Today, I'm focused on learning how data can help businesses make smarter decisions. Through my internship with Superior Construction this summer, I've been able to see how analytics can improve project planning and support better business outcomes.

My goal is to become a data analyst within a law firm, combining my legal background with my analytics skills. Much of that work involves spreadsheets and document-heavy processes, so I'm learning to code to complete those tasks more quickly and efficiently. I currently know R and Gephi, and I'm expanding into Python to build that foundation.

When I'm not working on projects, you can find me taking photos, traveling, or spending time with friends. I enjoy expressing my creativity with analytical thinking.
